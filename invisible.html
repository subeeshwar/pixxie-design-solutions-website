INVISIBLE MANNEQUIN

Show the Product.
Hide the Mannequin.

Invisible mannequin editing creates a clean, professional look that allows clothing and apparel to be displayed as if they were worn, without showing the model or mannequin. At Pixxie Design Solutions, we carefully remove the visible mannequin while preserving the natural shape, structure, and important details of the garment.

Our editing process maintains collars, sleeves, necklines, seams, folds, and other essential details to create a realistic hollow-man effect. This makes your apparel images look clean and consistent while keeping the focus entirely on the product.
